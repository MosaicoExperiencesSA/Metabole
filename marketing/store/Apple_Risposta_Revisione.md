Hello, thank you for the review. Here are the clarifications for both points.

**Guideline 2.1(a) — Demo account**
The demo account provided in App Review Information is fully functional and now contains pre-populated content: an active plan with ready daily menus, recipes, progress diary, coach chat and calendar are all visible immediately after login. Please re-test with the same credentials (in App Review Information). If a screen ever shows a "menu coming soon" message, pull down to refresh — the daily menu is now available.

**Guideline 2.1(b) — Business model**
1) Who uses the paid services: end clients who purchase a personalized nutrition coaching program. The same app is also used by our own coaches and nutritionists (staff accounts) to follow their clients.
2) Where they purchase: the coaching program is sold OUTSIDE the app — on our website metabole.eu and through direct arrangement with our human coaches. Payment is processed externally (Stripe / bank transfer). Nothing is sold inside the app.
3) What a user accesses in the app: after purchasing the program outside the app, the user accesses the delivery interface of that real-world human service — personalized daily menus, progress tracking, chat with their assigned human coach and nutritionist, and appointment scheduling.
4) Paid features unlocked without In-App Purchase: the personalized coaching program is a HUMAN service performed by real coaches and nutritionists, plus optional physical products (food supplements). No digital-only content is unlocked. Per guidelines 3.1.3(e) (services consumed outside the app) and 3.1.5 (physical goods), these do not require In-App Purchase.
5) Physical goods + services: users purchase a human coaching service and, optionally, physical products, consumed in the real world outside the app. There is no standalone digital content bundled. The app is solely the tool to follow the real-world program.

We remain available for any further information. Thank you.
